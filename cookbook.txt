## Classic Chocolate Fudge Cake
A rich, decadent chocolate cake perfect for celebrations.
- Category: Dessert, Baking
- Prep time: 20 mins | Cook time: 35 mins | Servings: 8 | Calories: 380 kcal/serving
- Diet Tags: Vegetarian, Contains Dairy, Contains Gluten, Contains Eggs

Ingredients:
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup whole milk (or buttermilk)
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 2 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
- 1 cup boiling water or fresh hot brewed coffee

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and lightly flour two 9-inch round cake pans.
2. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
3. Add the milk, vegetable oil, eggs, and vanilla extract. Beat on medium speed for 2 minutes until smooth.
4. Reduce speed and carefully stir in the boiling water or hot coffee. The batter will be thin, which creates a moist crumb.
5. Pour batter evenly into prepared cake pans.
6. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes until a toothpick inserted into center comes out clean.
7. Cool in pans for 10 minutes, then invert onto wire racks to cool completely before frosting.

## Healthy Vegan Banana Bread
Naturally sweetened, moist, plant-based banana bread made with whole grains.
- Category: Breakfast, Snack, Vegan Baking
- Prep time: 15 mins | Cook time: 50 mins | Servings: 10 | Calories: 190 kcal/serving
- Diet Tags: Vegan, Dairy-Free, Egg-Free, Plant-Based

Ingredients:
- 3 large overripe bananas, thoroughly mashed
- 1/3 cup melted virgin coconut oil (or olive oil)
- 1/2 cup organic coconut sugar or pure maple syrup
- 1/4 cup unsweetened almond milk (or oat milk)
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 cups whole wheat pastry flour (or gluten-free 1-to-1 baking flour)
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
- Optional: 1/2 cup chopped walnuts or dark chocolate chips

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a 9x5-inch loaf pan with parchment paper.
2. In a large bowl, whisk mashed bananas, melted coconut oil, sweetener, almond milk, and vanilla until smooth.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt.
4. Fold dry ingredients into wet ingredients until just combined. Do not overmix. Fold in nuts or chocolate if desired.
5. Transfer batter to loaf pan and smooth top. Bake for 48 to 55 minutes until golden brown and a skewer comes out clean.
6. Cool in pan for 15 minutes, then cool on a wire rack before slicing.

## Creamy Garlic Herb Tuscan Chicken
Tender chicken breasts in a rich garlic, sundried tomato, and spinach cream sauce.
- Category: Main Course, Dinner, Italian-American
- Prep time: 10 mins | Cook time: 20 mins | Servings: 4 | Calories: 440 kcal/serving
- Diet Tags: High-Protein, Keto-Friendly, Low-Carb, Gluten-Free

Ingredients:
- 2 large chicken breasts, sliced horizontally into 4 cutlets
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- Salt and freshly cracked black pepper to taste
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes, drained and sliced
- 1 cup heavy cream (or unsweetened coconut cream for dairy-free)
- 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 cups fresh baby spinach

Instructions:
1. Season chicken cutlets with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and Italian seasoning.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken for 5-6 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through (internal temp 165°F). Transfer to a plate.
3. In the same skillet, add minced garlic and sun-dried tomatoes. Sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Pour in chicken broth and heavy cream; bring to a gentle simmer.
5. Stir in grated Parmesan cheese until melted and smooth.
6. Add baby spinach and cook for 1-2 minutes until wilted.
7. Return chicken cutlets to skillet and spoon sauce over top. Simmer for 2 minutes and serve warm with pasta, rice, or zucchini noodles.

## Mediterranean Quinoa Power Bowl
A nutrient-dense, colorful bowl packed with fiber, healthy fats, and vibrant herbs.
- Category: Lunch, Salad, Mediterranean
- Prep time: 15 mins | Cook time: 15 mins | Servings: 4 | Calories: 320 kcal/serving
- Diet Tags: Vegetarian, Vegan-Adaptable, Gluten-Free, High-Fiber

Ingredients:
- 1 cup uncooked quinoa (rinsed) cooked in 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 cup canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup English cucumber, diced
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 cup Kalamata olives, pitted and sliced
- 1/3 cup red onion, finely chopped
- 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese (omit for vegan)
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley and mint, finely chopped

Lemon Herb Dressing:
- 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. Cook quinoa in vegetable broth according to package directions. Fluff with a fork and allow to cool.
2. In a small jar, whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper.
3. In a large serving bowl, assemble cooled quinoa, chickpeas, cucumbers, cherry tomatoes, olives, and red onion.
4. Drizzle dressing over the bowl and toss gently.
5. Top with crumbled feta cheese and fresh herbs before serving.
